# 🎵 Music Player Configuration

The music player plays your playlist, or controls the background video when the video is the sound. The [Config Builder](https://loadingscreen.4zam.dev/builder.html) does this for you under **Music**, with a live preview of the real loading screen.

[Open the Config Builder](https://loadingscreen.4zam.dev/builder.html){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
[Build with AI](https://loadingscreen.4zam.dev/ai.html){ .md-button }

Editing `config.json` by hand? Every option is in the Config reference: [`music`](config-reference.md#music), [`defaultVolume`](config-reference.md#defaultVolume) and [`videoAsAudio`](config-reference.md#videoAsAudio).
